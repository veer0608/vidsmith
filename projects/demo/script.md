# Why Your Bank Statement Lies

## The hook
[visual: paper bank statement on a desk]
Your bank statement is not a record of what you spent. It is a record of what your bank found convenient to store.

## Merchant names
[visual: card terminal in a small shop]
The merchant name on a transaction is typed by the payment processor, not the shop. That is why a coffee costs money at a company you have never heard of.

## Dates drift
[visual: calendar pages turning]
The date you see is usually the settlement date, not the day you paid. On a weekend, those can be three days apart.

## What to do
[visual: person reconciling receipts]
Reconcile against receipts, not memory. If the two disagree, the receipt is almost always right.
