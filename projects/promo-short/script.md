# vidsmith Short

## The hook
[visual: person typing on a laptop at night]
You write a script in a plain text file. This turns it into a narrated video, with stock footage and captions that land on the exact word being spoken.

## Where the timings come from
[diagram: caption timing from the speech engine versus transcribing it back]
Most tools transcribe the audio back to work out where the words landed. This one takes the timings straight from the speech engine that made the sound, so nothing drifts.

## It runs on your machine
[visual: desktop computer running in a home office]
The render happens on your own hardware. There is no charge per video, no credits to buy, and no monthly bill that keeps running after you stop.

## What it costs
[visual: laptop and a coffee cup on a desk in morning light]
Free for personal use. Commercial work needs a one off licence, not a subscription. The source is public on GitHub.
